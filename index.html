About Me

Turning complex data
into human impact

I'm a Senior Decision Scientist at CVS Health, where I use AI and analytics to solve real-world problems within healthcare. My work focuses on making complex business decisions easier through predictive modeling, optimization, and innovative use of AI. Whether it's streamlining processes or enhancing patient experiences, I aim to make a tangible impact with every project I undertake.

Previously, I was a Management Consultant at Kearney (Digital and Analytics), where I specialized in transforming intricate challenges into actionable insights. My experience with Generative AI and Natural Language Processing (NLP) has been instrumental in pushing the boundaries of business applications in these emerging fields. I'm particularly passionate about Large Language Models, optimization techniques, and the practical deployment of AI solutions.

Outside of work, I'm an avid traveler and a foodie who loves to explore new places and cultures. So far, I've visited Spain, the United States, Japan, Mexico, South Korea, China, Singapore, Indonesia (Bali), the United Kingdom, the Czech Republic, Austria, United Arab Emirates (Dubai), and Thailand! I also enjoy painting, cooking, playing board games, Nintendo, and badminton.

Fun fact: I am a proud Google Maps Level 7 Local Guide, and my photos have garnered over 10 million views.
